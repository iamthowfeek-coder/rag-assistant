# Account and Passwords

## Reset your password
Go to the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter your email address and we will send a reset link. The link is valid for 1 hour. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.

## Change your email address
Sign in, open Account Settings, and choose Change email. We send a confirmation link to the new address. The change is complete once you click it.

## Delete your account
Email us from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and personal data within 14 days. Order history required for tax records is kept for 7 years.

## Two-step verification
You can turn on two-step verification in Account Settings. We support authenticator apps. SMS codes are not available.
