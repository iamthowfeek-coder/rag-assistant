Warranty and Repairs

All products include a 12-month limited warranty that starts on the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover damage caused by drops, water, or unauthorized repairs.

To make a warranty claim, email us your order number and a short description or video of the problem. Our team replies within 2 business days with the next steps.

If a product is covered, we will repair it or send a replacement. Repairs usually take 10 business days from the day we receive the item. We pay for shipping on approved warranty claims.

Out-of-warranty repairs are available for a flat fee of 25 dollars plus parts. We send a quote before starting any paid repair.

Battery replacement is offered for 15 dollars after the warranty has ended.
