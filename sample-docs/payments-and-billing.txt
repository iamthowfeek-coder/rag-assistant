Payments and Billing

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not accept cash on delivery or cheques.

Your card is charged when your order ships, not when you place it. If an order is split into two shipments, you are charged separately for each shipment.

Invoices are emailed after each charge. You can also download invoices from Order History in your account. To add a company name or tax number to an invoice, email us within 30 days of the order.

If you see a charge you do not recognize, contact us with the last 4 digits of the card and the date. We investigate within 3 business days.

Prices are shown in US dollars. Your bank may add a currency conversion fee.
