Contact and Support Hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Eastern Time. We are closed on weekends and on public holidays.

Emails sent to support@example.com receive a reply within 1 business day. Messages sent outside support hours are answered on the next working day.

We do not offer phone support or live chat. Email is the fastest way to reach us, and every email gets a ticket number so nothing is lost.

For urgent problems with an order that has not shipped yet, put the word URGENT at the start of your email subject. Urgent emails are answered first, usually within 4 business hours.
