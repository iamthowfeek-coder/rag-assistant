Shipping FAQ

How long does delivery take?
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs extra.

Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to more than 40 countries. International orders take 7 to 14 business days and customers pay any import duties.

How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you receive an email with a tracking link. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.

What if my package is lost?
If your tracking has not moved for 7 days, contact us and we will send a replacement or refund the order.
