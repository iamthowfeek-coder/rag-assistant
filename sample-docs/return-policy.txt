Return Policy

You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories included.

To start a return, reply to your order confirmation email with the word RETURN and your order number. We will send you a prepaid return label within one business day.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. After we receive the item, the refund takes 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement.

Items damaged on arrival can be replaced free of charge. Send us a photo of the damage within 7 days of delivery.
